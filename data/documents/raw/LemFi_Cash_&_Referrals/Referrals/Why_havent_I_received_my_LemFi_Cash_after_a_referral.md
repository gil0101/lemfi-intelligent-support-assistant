# Why haven't I received my LemFi Cash after a referral?

If your LemFi Cash hasn't appeared, your friend's qualifying transfer may not have been completed yet.

**What this means**

Your referral LemFi Cash is released when your friend completes the following:

• Signed up using your referral code or link

• Completed a single qualifying cross-border transfer above the minimum amount for their country

LemFi Cash will be available only once those steps are both complete. The bonus is not triggered by sign-up alone.

**What you should do**

• Check that your friend used your code at sign-up or at the referrals section in-app.

• Ask your friend to confirm they've completed a qualifying transfer, not just added money to their account

• If all steps are complete and you still haven't received it, contact us via the in-app Help section

**What to expect**

• If everything checks out, your LemFi Cash will appear automatically

• Bonus payments aren't instant in all cases — allow a short period after your friend's transfer completes
