# How much LemFi Cash do I earn for a referral?

You will earn Lemfi Cash** **each time someone you refer completes a qualifying transfer. The amount is based on the country you're registered in and can vary - you can see how much you will earn in the LemFi cash

**What this means**

• Your reward is always in your own currency, from your own country's offer

• If your friend lives in a different country, that doesn't affect your amount — you still earn the standard amount for your market

• Amounts may vary during special promotions

**What to expect**

• Referral bonuses can change over time. Check the Referrals section in the app or the Help Center to get the latest updates.

• Please note that LemFi Cash expires — make sure you use it on time!
