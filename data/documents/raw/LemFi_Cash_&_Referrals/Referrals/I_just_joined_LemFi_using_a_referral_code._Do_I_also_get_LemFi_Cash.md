# I just joined LemFi using a referral code. Do I also get LemFi Cash?

Yes — when you sign up with a referral code, you'll receive LemFi Cash too. It's a reward for both you and the person who invited you.

**What this means**

• Your LemFi Cash is credited to your wallet as soon as you sign up with a valid referral code

• You receive it on sign-up and do not need to complete a transfer first.

• Your LemFi Cash is in your own home currency (the currency of the country you signed up from)

**What you should do**

• Make sure you enter the referral code during sign-up, not after

• Codes entered after account creation cannot be applied

**What to expect**

• Your LemFi Cash will be visible in your wallet immediately after signing up

• The amount you receive is based on the standard offer for your sign-up country

• Please note that LemFi Cash expires — make sure you use it on time!
