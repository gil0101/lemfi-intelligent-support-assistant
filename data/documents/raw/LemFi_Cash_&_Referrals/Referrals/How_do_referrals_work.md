# How do referrals work?

LemFi Cash is our new rewards program. When you invite a friend to LemFi and they make their first qualifying transfer, you'll earn LemFi Cash in your own currency, sitting in your LemFi Cash Wallet ready to use your money on your next transfer.

**What this means**

• Share your unique referral code or link from the LemFi Cash section in the app

• Your friend signs up and makes a qualifying cross-border transfer (a minimum amount can vary by country)

• Once they do, your LemFi Cash lands in your wallet automatically — no action needed from you

• Your LemFi Cash is in your home currency - ready to use!

**What you should do**

• Find your referral code in the app

• Share it with family and friends — **the more you share, the more you can earn!**

• There's no limit on how many people you can refer

• Your link/code never changes or expires; you can use it in your social media posts.

**What to expect**

• Your LemFi Cash won't appear until your friend completes their qualifying transfer

• You'll be notified as soon as it lands

• Please note that LemFi Cash expires — make sure you use it on time!
