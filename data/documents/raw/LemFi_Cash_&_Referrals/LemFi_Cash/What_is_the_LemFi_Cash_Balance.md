# What is the LemFi Cash Balance?

The LemFi Cash Balance serves as a centralized hub for all credits earned through referrals, rewards, promotional offers, and one-time migrations from LemFi Points.

This works by having your balance denominated in your home currency, and credits are automatically applied to boost the cost of your next eligible international transfer.

Think of it as a continuous balance that grows as you interact with our services, ready to be used whenever you send money. We are continuously expanding the LemFi ecosystem, and this balance will soon apply to a wider range of LemFi services.
