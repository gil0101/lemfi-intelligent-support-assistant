# What is happening to my LemFi Points?

LemFi Points are being upgraded to LemFi Cash. Your full points balance will automatically be converted into LemFi Cash and deposited into your new LemFi Cash wallet upon updating the app. You don't need to do anything.

**What this means**

• Every 100 points becomes 1 GBP, 1 USD, 1 CAD, or 1 EUR — depending on your home currency

• Your converted balance appears in your wallet as a single LemFi Cash credit

• This converted credit **never expires**

• Points earning will switch off once you update the app to Lemfi Cash. From that point onwards, you will earn LemFi Cash instead.

**What you should do**

• No action is required from you

**What to expect**

• Your converted LemFi Cash will be visible in your wallet from app version xx onwards.

• The total value will match your points balance exactly — nothing is lost in the conversion

• Even small balances are converted. If your balance is below the minimum spend threshold, it will still be in your wallet and will contribute toward a discount when combined with future LemFi Cash
