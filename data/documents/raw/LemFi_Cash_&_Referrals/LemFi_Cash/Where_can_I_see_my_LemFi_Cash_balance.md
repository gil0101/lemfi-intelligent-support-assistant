# Where can I see my LemFi Cash balance?

You can easily view and manage your LemFi Cash balance directly within the mobile app.

**What you should do**

• Open the LemFi app on your device.

• Navigate to the LemFi Cash Wallet from your home screen

**What to expect**

•
**Total Balance:** View the complete summary of your current promotional funds.

•
**Credit Breakdown:** Tap on any individual credit to see its specific details, including the amount, its source, and the expiry date (if applicable).

•
**Transaction History:** Access a full statement showing how your balance has been earned and spent over time.
