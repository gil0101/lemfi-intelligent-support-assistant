# How do I earn LemFi Cash?

There are several ways to accumulate LemFi Cash:

•
**Referrals:** Earn cash by successfully referring friends or by being referred yourself. This is a one-time payment. While most rewards are credited automatically, some referral programs may require you to enter a specific referral code during signup.

•
**Promotional Offers**: Keep an eye out for special seasonal or targeted promotions.

•
**Transaction points:** This applies only when a qualifying international transfer amount is sent to a specific destination country.
