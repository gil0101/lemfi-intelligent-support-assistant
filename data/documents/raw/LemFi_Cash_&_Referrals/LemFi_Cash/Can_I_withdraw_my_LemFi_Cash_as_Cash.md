# Can I withdraw my LemFi Cash as Cash?

No. LemFi Cash is a promotional balance and does not behave like a regular wallet balance.

**Important to know:**

• It cannot be withdrawn.

• You cannot transfer it to another user or account and it cannot be converted to cash.

• It can strictly be used as a boost on eligible international transfers.
