# How do I use my LemFi Cash?

Using your rewards is completely seamless. When you initiate an eligible cross-border transfer, your LemFi Cash balance is automatically applied to boost your transfer value. You will always see the exact boost amount on your screen before you confirm the transaction.

**What this means**

• This is applicable only to international transfers from your home currency (e.g., a GBP transfer to Africa, Asia and other cross-border countries)

• The precise boost amount is dynamically calculated based on the real-time exchange rate of your recipient's currency.

• Same-currency transfers (e.g., a GBP to GBP) are not eligible for LemFi Cash.

**What you should do**

• Go to the final transfer summary screen before confirming.

• Check the displayed LemFi Cash boost to confirm the final amount.

• The buttons are automatically toggled or set to turn on; eligible credits apply themselves automatically.

• Your transaction receipt will clearly break down the original transfer amount, your LemFi Cash discount, and your final total paid.

• If you hold multiple separate LemFi Cash credits, the system automatically uses the credit closest to its expiration date first.
