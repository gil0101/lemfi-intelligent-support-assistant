# How do I set up biometric login?

Setting up biometrics (Face ID/fingerprint) on LemFi enhances security for your funds and provides faster account access, helping protect your account from unauthorized access.


**What this means**

Biometrics let you sign in using your device’s built-in security features instead of entering your password each time.


**What you should do**

• Log in to your account

• Click the profile icon at the top-left corner of the page

• Select "Security and privacy."

• Click on Enable/disable biometrics

• Follow the prompts to set up your login.

**What to expect**

• You’ll be able to log in quickly using your fingerprint or face ID

• Your biometric data stays on your device and is not shared


**Next steps**

Make sure your device supports biometric authentication and is properly set up.
