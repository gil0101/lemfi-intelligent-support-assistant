# I forgot my password

If you’ve forgotten your password, you can reset it quickly and securely.


**What this means**

You’ll need to verify your identity before creating a new password.



**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Tap "Recover account."

• If your credentials have been previously saved, select "Recover Account" on the login page

•
Choose "Forgot your password."

• Input your phone number

• Enter the one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered email address

• If you are prompted to undergo facial verification, please complete this step

• Once successful, create a new password and tap"Continue" to finish the password reset process


**What to expect**

You’ll be guided through a secure process to create a new password.
