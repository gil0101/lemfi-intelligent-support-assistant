# I’m trying to log in on a new device

If you’re logging in on a new device, you may need to complete extra security checks.


**What this means**

For your safety, we may:

• Send a verification code

• Request additional checks, including Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

**What you should do**

• Enter your login details

• Complete any verification steps

• Make sure your contact details are accessible

**What to expect**

You may be asked to confirm your identity before accessing your account.
