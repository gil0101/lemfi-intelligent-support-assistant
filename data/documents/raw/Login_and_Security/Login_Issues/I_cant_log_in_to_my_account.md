# I can’t log in to my account

If you’re having trouble logging in, don’t worry, this is usually easy to fix.


**What you should do**

You may be unable to log in for many reasons. If your account is not restricted due to a regulatory review;

• Check that your phone number is correct

• Make sure your internet connection is stable

• Verify that you are not connected to a VPN.

• Restart the app and try again

• Reset your password if needed

**What to expect**

Most login issues can be resolved quickly by retrying and/or checking the details above.
