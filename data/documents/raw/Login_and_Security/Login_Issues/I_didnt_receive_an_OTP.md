# I didn’t receive an OTP

If you didn’t receive your OTP to reset your password, it may be delayed.


**What this means**

OTPs can sometimes be delayed for the following reasons:

• Network issues with your provider

• Your email address is unverified

• You have unsubscribed from our mailing list

**What you should do**

• Wait a few moments and request a new code

• Check that you are not connected to VPN

• Check your email spam or junk folder

• Make sure your phone has internet connectivity

• Confirm your email details are correct

**What to expect**

OTPs usually arrive within a few seconds, but delays can happen occasionally.
