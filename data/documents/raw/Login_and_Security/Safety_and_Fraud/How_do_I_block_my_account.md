# How do I block my account?

You can request a block on your account to prevent unauthorized access.



**What this means**

When your account is blocked, you will be:

• unable to log in to your account

• unable to carry out transactions

**What you should do**

• Request an account block

• Follow any security steps provided

**What to expect**

• Your account will be secured

• Your account will be restricted until stated otherwise

**Next steps**

Once your account is safe, you can work with the support team to restore your access.
