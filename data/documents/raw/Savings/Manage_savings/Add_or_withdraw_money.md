# Add or withdraw money

You can add to or withdraw from your savings account at any time.


**What this means**

You can move funds between the following:

• your LemFi wallet

• your savings account

Funds must be transferred through your wallet (not directly from or to an external bank account).

**What you should do**

**To add money:**

• Log in to your LemFi app.

• Select "Savings" under the Services section of the homepage.

• Tap "Add" below your balance.

• Enter the amount you want to deposit

• If you would like to add funds directly from your linked bank account, click on "Change" and select "Instant bank transfer".

• Follow the prompts

**To withdraw money:**

• Go to your **Savings** account

• Tap **Move**

• Enter the amount

• Confirm the withdrawal to return funds to your main wallet.

**What to expect**

• You can start saving from as little as **£1 (maximum transaction depends on your GBP wallet transaction limit)**

• Deposits are processed quickly and begin earning interest shortly after

• Withdrawals are free and usually arrive in your wallet instantly

In some cases, withdrawals may take longer due to processing checks
