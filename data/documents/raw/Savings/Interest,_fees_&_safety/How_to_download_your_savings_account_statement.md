# How to download your savings account statement

You can download your LemFi savings statement directly from the app.

**What this means**

LemFi makes your savings account statements available in the app for easy access at any time. This document reflects your full savings account history and can be used for personal records or tax reporting.


**What you should do**

**To download your statement:**

• Open the app

• Navigate to your savings account

• Click on the three dots at the top-right corner

• Tap **More**

• Select **Statement**

• Choose the period for which you would like to generate a statement

• Click **Generate **and it will be sent to your registered email address

**What to expect**

• Your statement will be sent to your registered email address

• Your statement will cover the desired period chosen

• It will include your transaction history and interest earned
