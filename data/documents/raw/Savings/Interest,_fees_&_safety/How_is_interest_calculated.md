# How is interest calculated?

The Instant Access Savings Account offers a competitive 3.92% AER (variable) tracker rate, paid monthly, with no monthly maintenance fees or penalties. The account is secured by ClearBank, with eligible deposits protected up to £120,000 by the UK Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).


**What this means**

Your account is structured so that your savings are kept separately from LemFi’s operating funds. Funds are held by ClearBank, a regulated UK financial institution, and protected by the FSCS up to £120,000. Interest is calculated based on your savings balance and a variable rate that tracks below the Bank of England base rate.

Interest:

• accrues daily

• is paid out monthly

Because the rate is a tracker rate, LemFI may change the interest rate in line with the Bank of England's base rate.

**Important to know **

You may be responsible for paying tax on interest earned depending on your personal circumstances. Please seek professional tax advice if you are unsure about your tax position
