# Are there any fees on LemFi savings?

No, there are no fees for using the LemFi Instant Access Savings account. The account, available to UK residents and powered by ClearBank, features zero maintenance fees, no penalties for withdrawals, and no minimum balance requirements.


**What this means**

There are no fees for:

• opening a savings account

• depositing money

• withdrawing money

**Important to know **

• No hidden charges and no charges for deposits or withdrawals.

• Currently offers a competitive variable interest rate paid on your balance.

• Instant access to funds without breakage fees.

• Eligible deposits are protected by the FSCS up to £120,000.
