# What is the FSCS?

The U.K. Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) is an independent body that compensates authorized financial services firms’ users up to its compensation limit of £120,000 per person in the event such firms fail. The Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) is a free service that was set up in 2001 and is funded by the financial services industry.
