# What is a Tracker Rate?

LemFi Savings offers a tracker rate, which means your interest rate automatically follows the Bank of England Base Rate.

If the BoE raises rates, your savings rate goes up automatically.

If the BoE cuts rates, your savings rate will adjust downward, but your "Boost" or "Margin" remains the same.
