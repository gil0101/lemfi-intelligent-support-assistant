# How do I open a savings account?

Opening a savings account is quick and easy

You can create a savings account directly in the app if you’re eligible.


**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Go to **Home**

• Select “**Savings” **under the services section

• Tap **Add new savings**

• Follow the steps to set up your account

**What to expect**

• You’ll need to accept the savings terms and conditions

• You can open one savings account at a time

Your account will be ready once setup is complete
