# How does the 6 month bonus rate work?

Your bonus is a fixed 1.96% boost added directly to our tracker rate. This gives you a total current rate of 5.00% AER.

Your 6-month (183-day) bonus period kicks off the second you activate the Bonus Plan in your app.
