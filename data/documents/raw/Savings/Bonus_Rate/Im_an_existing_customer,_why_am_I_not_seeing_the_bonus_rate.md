# I’m an existing customer, why am I not seeing the bonus rate?

Current customers must manually upgrade to the Bonus Plan via the app. We don’t switch you automatically because the 183-day countdown only starts when you’re ready. Just review the terms we’ve sent by email, opt-in, and your 5.00% AER rate kicks in instantly.

If you can't see the terms to opt in yet, please note that it is being gradually rolled out.
