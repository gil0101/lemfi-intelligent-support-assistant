# What are my wallet limits?

LemFi wallets have no limits on how much you can hold, but there are limits on how much can be funded into your wallet.


**What this means**

Your wallet can hold any amount of funds, but there are limits to the amount that can be funded into your wallet that are set based on:

• Your account verification level

•  Local regulations

• Your bank regulations

**What you should do**

Check your wallet limits in the app or during a transaction.

You can also follow these steps to view your limits on the LemFi app:

• Log in to your account

• Click the profile icon to access your settings

• Click "Account Information"

• Click "Account Limits"



**What to expect**

• Limits may increase after additional verification

You may be restricted if limits are exceeded
