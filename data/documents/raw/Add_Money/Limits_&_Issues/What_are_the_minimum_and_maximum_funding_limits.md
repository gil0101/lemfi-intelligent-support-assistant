# What are the minimum and maximum funding limits?

There are limits on how much you can add to your account.


**What this means**

Your limits may depend on:

• your verification level

• your location

• regulatory requirements

**What you should do**

Check your limits in the app:

• Log in to the app

• Scroll down on the homepage to “Account information”

• view all limits

**What to expect**

• If you input an amount that exceeds your limit, you would be unable to go forward with the transaction

• You may need to adjust the amount or complete additional verification to increase your limit
