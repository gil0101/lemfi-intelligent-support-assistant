# Why is my deposit taking time?

If your deposit is taking longer than expected, it may still be processing.


**What this means**

Processing times depend on:

• The payment method used

• the sending bank’s processing time


If your bank has already deducted the money, but it hasn’t appeared in your account yet, it’s usually still being processed.


**What you should do**

• Check your payment status in the app

• Allow extra time if the payment was made outside working hours

**What to expect**

• Some deposits are instant

• Others may take 24 hours to be credited.
