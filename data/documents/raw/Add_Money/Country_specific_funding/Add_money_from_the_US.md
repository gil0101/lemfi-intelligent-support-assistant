# Add money from the US

You can add money to your account using supported US payment methods.

**What this means**

Funding options may include debit cards, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, depending on availability.


**What you should do**

Open the app, tap “Add money”, and choose your preferred method.

Select “Add" and choose your preferred payment method

**For Debit Card**

• Select "Debit Card" to continue

• Input the amount you wish to deposit and click "Continue."

• Enter relevant information in the fields displayed and follow the prompt to complete the process successfully

**For Google Pay/Apple Pay**

• Select "Google Pay” (for Android) OR Apple Pay (on iOS)

• Enter the desired amount, click “Continue,” and proceed with the process.

Please ensure you authorize the transaction with your bank to avoid any interruptions during the process.

**What to expect**

• Successful payments are credited instantly.
