# Add money from Kenya

**What this means**

This means you request money via the mobile money option from your contacts in Kenya.

**What you should do**

Open the app and select "Add money” under the KES wallet

• Select "Add with mobile money."

• Input the amount that you want to add

• Add a new recipient or search from your enabled contacts

• Fill all required fields correctly (first and last names, mobile money operator, and phone number)

• Review details and send a payment request

• A prompt will be sent to the phone number to authorize payment with PIN

• Once payment is authorized, the money will be deposited into your KES wallet, and you will receive a notification

• Ensure that the mobile money phone number is active and turned on so that the prompt can be delivered successfully.

**What to expect**

Most payments are processed instantly. If you experience a delay, contact the support team with the following information:

• A screenshot of the proof of debit (should include the date and amount funded
