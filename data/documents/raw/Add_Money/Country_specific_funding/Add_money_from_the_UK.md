# Add money from the UK

Adding money from the UK is simple, and we support multiple payment methods.


**What this means**

You can fund your account using payment methods available in the UK, such as debit cards, instant bank transfer, virtual funding, and Apple Pay or Google Pay.


**What you should do**

Go to "Add money" in the app and select any payment method of your choice.

For the “**Debit card**” method

• Enter the amount you wish to deposit, then click "Continue."

• Enter your card information (card number, expiration date, and CVV)

• Follow the prompt to add desired funds successfully

• Kindly ensure you authorize this transaction from your bank so that the process can be completed.

For the “**instant bank transfer**” method

• Click "Verify your account."

• Follow the prompt

• You will be redirected to the TrueLayer page

• Select your preferred bank account and follow the prompt

• A confirmation would be sent to your bank app for your authorization

• Proceed to fund your wallet with your desired amount

For the “**virtual funding**” method

• Click on "Regular bank transfer," and your virtual account details will be displayed

• Send funds from your UK bank to the displayed details, and it will be deposited into your GBP wallet shortly after.

This funding method is only permitted with a UK bank registered in your name, as we do not support 3rd party payments to your virtual account.


For the “**Apple Pay, or Google Pay**” method

• Select "Google Pay” (for Android) OR Apple Pay (on iOS)

• Enter the desired amount, click “Continue,” and proceed with the process.

• Please ensure you authorize the transaction with your bank to avoid any interruptions during the process.

**What to expect**

• Debit card, virtual funding, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Instant bank transfer payments are instant

• Other payments, like funding from your UK bank to LemFi’s account, may take up to 24 hours
