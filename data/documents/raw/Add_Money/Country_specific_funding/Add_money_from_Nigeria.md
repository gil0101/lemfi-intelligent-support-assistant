# Add money from Nigeria

You can add money to your account from Nigeria using the bank transfer method.

**What this means**

This means you transfer money to the displayed account details on the app from a Nigerian bank account.

**What you should do**

Open the app and select "Add money" to view your unique account number.
Make a transfer from your Nigerian bank account to the provided details following the steps below:

• Click on "Add" and select “Regular Bank Transfer” as the available payment method

• Your account information will be displayed

• Send money to these details through a Nigerian bank account, and it will be deposited into your NGN wallet

**What to expect**

Most payments are processed quickly. If you experience a delay, contact the support team with the following information:

• A screenshot of the proof of debit

• The Paga account number to which the funds were sent

• The session ID (a 30-digit number)
