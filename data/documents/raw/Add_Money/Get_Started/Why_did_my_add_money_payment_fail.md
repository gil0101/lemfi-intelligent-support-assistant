#  Why did my add money payment fail?

If your payment failed, don’t worry — this can happen for a few common reasons.


**What this means**

LemFi add money failures often occur due to using an unauthorized third-party account, mismatched names between the bank and the app, or network issues.

Common causes include:

• insufficient funds

• Invalid payment methods

• incorrect payment details

• bank restrictions

• temporary system issues

**What you should do**

• Check your payment details and make sure your name matches

•  Have adequate funds in your bank or card

• Check the app status

• Try again or use a different payment method

**What to expect**

Retrying the payment or choosing another method can resolve most issues.
