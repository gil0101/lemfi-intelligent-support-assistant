# Opening Multi-Currency Wallets

Users within the United Kingdom, United States, or Canada can open additional currency wallets asides those in the currency of the sign-up countries (GBP, USD or CAD). To do this:

• Log into the LemFi app

• On the homepage, select "Tap to Add account"; alternatively, click on the three-dotted icon close to the "Add Money" button

• A list of wallets in various African currencies will be displayed

• Choose your preferred currency choice (whether Nigerian Naira or Kenyan Shillings)

• A wallet in the selected currency will be created instantly
