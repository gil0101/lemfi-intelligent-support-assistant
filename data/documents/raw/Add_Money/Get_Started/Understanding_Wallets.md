# Understanding Wallets

As a LemFi user, a wallet enables you to receive and hold money in your LemFi account. Depending on your sign-up region, a wallet in the currency of your registration country may be assigned to you upon account opening, whether pounds (GBP), U.S. dollars (USD) or Canadian dollars (CAD).

Other types of wallets available for U.K., U.S., or Canadian customers are the Nigerian Naira (NGN) and Kenyan Shilling (KES) wallets. Both wallets allow migrants from the African plane to receive money in any of these currencies into the LemFi accounts.

*N.B. Wallet options are currently unavailable for European users at this time*.
