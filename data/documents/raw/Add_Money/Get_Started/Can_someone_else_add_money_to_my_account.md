# Can someone else add money to my account?

No, other people cannot directly add money to your LemFi account. LemFi requires that funds come from a bank account, card, or payment method held in the same name as your LemFi account to prevent third-party funding.

However, customers with a Global account or PAGA account can accept third-party funding. A LemFi user can send money directly to another customer using the in-app “LemFi Friends” feature if both have wallets.

**What this means**

For security reasons, payments should usually come from accounts in your name. You must not share your account login details or allow anyone else to access your account to make payments on your behalf.

**What you should do**

Ensure that you are certain of the following:

• the payment method belongs to you

• the details match your account information

• You own a global account

**What to expect**

• Payments from third-party accounts may be declined

In some cases, additional checks may be required.
