# What payment methods can I use?

You can fund your account using different payment methods depending on your location. However, LemFi does not accept credit cards as a payment method.


**What this means**

Available payment methods include:

Canada:

• Interac Autodeposit

• Interac Request Money

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

UK:

• Debit cards

• Instant bank transfer

• Virtual funding

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

US:

• Debit cards

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

Nigeria:

• Bank transfer

Kenya:

• Mobile money

**What you should do**

Click on "Add money" in the app to see the payment methods available to you.


**What to expect**

• Available options may vary by country

• Some methods are instant, while others take longer
