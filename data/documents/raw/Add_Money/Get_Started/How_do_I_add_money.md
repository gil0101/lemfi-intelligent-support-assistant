# How do I add money?

Adding money to your LemFi wallet is quick and straightforward.


**What this means**

You can add funds to your wallet using supported payment methods available in your country. LemFi does not accept credit card funding and does not charge fees to add money to your wallet.

Payment methods available by country can be found below:

Canada:

• Interac Autodeposit

• Interac Request Money

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

UK:

• Debit cards

• Instant bank transfer

• Virtual funding

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

US:

• Debit cards

• Apple Pay

• Google Pay

Nigeria:

• Bank transfer

Kenya:

• mobile money

**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Tap "Add money"

• Select your payment method

• Enter the amount

• Review and confirm the payment

**What to expect**

• Your balance will update once the payment is successful

• Some payment methods are instant, while others may take a little longer
