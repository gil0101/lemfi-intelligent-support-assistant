# Can I get a receipt?

Yes, you can get a receipt for transactions on the LemFi app. Receipts for transfers are available directly within the app, and you can download monthly account statements in PDF or Excel format by navigating to the "Account information" section in your profile.

**What this means**

Every successful transfer includes a digital receipt you can access anytime.

**What you should do**

To download your receipt:

• Open the app

• Go to Transactions

• Select the transfer

• Tap "Download" beside “statement”

**What to expect**

Your receipt has a “sender's copy” and “recipient's copy," which includes

• transaction details

• amount sent

• fees and exchange rate

• date and time

**Next steps**

You can use your receipt as proof of payment or for your records.
