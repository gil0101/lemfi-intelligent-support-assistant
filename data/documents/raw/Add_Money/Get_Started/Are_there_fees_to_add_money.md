# Are there fees to add money?

No, LemFi does not charge a fee to add money to your LemFi wallet. Deposits are generally free, allowing you to add funds via available payment methods without incurring additional costs.

However:

• Your bank or card provider may apply their own charges

**What this means **

Always review the payment summary before confirming your deposit.


**What to expect**

• You’ll see any applicable fees before completing your payment

• There are no hidden charges after confirmation
