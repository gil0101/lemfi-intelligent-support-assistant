# Is my device compatible for eSIM?

Your device must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked.


**What this means**

To use eSIM:

• Your device must support eSIM

• Your device must not be locked to a network

**What you should do**

• Dial *#06# — if you see an EID, your device supports eSIM

• Check your settings to confirm your device is unlocked

**What to expect**

Most newer smartphones support eSIM.
