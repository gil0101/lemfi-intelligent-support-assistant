# What is LemFi eSIM?

LemFi eSIM is a digital data plan service that allows users to access international mobile data directly on their phones without a physical SIM card, enabling affordable, high-speed data connectivity for immigrants and travelers.

**What this means**

An eSIM is a digital SIM that you can install directly on your device.

With LemFi eSIM, you can:

• stay connected in multiple countries

• avoid roaming charges

• switch plans easily from the app

**What you should do**

Choose and install a plan in the app before or during your trip.


**What to expect**

• Your eSIM works alongside your existing SIM

You can use your primary SIM for calls and texts, and eSIM for data
