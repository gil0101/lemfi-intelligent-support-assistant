# Where can I use LemFi eSIM?

You can use LemFi eSIM in more than 76 countries worldwide.


**What this means**

eSIM plans are available across regions, including:

• Europe

• Africa

• Asia

• North and South America

**What you should do**

Verify available destinations in the app when selecting your plan.

**What to expect**

• Supported countries may vary by plan

You’ll see coverage before purchasing
