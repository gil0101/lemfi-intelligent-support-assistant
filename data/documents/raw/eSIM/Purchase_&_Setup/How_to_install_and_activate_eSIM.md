# How to install and activate eSIM

Once you’ve purchased a plan, you can install your eSIM.



**What this means**

Installation connects your device to your eSIM plan.


**What you should do**

• Install your eSIM through the app

• Follow on-screen instructions

Before travel:

• install your eSIM

• keep roaming OFF

At destination:

• turn on data roaming

• select your eSIM for mobile data

**What to expect**

• Activation usually takes a few minutes

Your plan starts when you connect abroad
