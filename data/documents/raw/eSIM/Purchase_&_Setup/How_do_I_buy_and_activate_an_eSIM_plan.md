# How do I buy and activate an eSIM plan?

You can purchase and activate your eSIM plan directly in the app.

**What this means**

eSIM plans are prepaid — you pay upfront before using data.

**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Go to **eSIM**

• Choose your destination and plan

• Confirm payment

• Install your eSIM

**What to expect**

• Your plan activates when you connect to a network at your destination

No unexpected roaming charges
