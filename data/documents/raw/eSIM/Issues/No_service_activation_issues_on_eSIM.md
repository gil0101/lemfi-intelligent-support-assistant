# No service / activation issues  on eSIM

If your eSIM has no service, it may be due to network or setup issues.


**What this means**

This can happen if:

• your plan hasn’t activated yet

• you’ve reached your data limit

• there are local network issues

**What you should do**

• check your data usage

• confirm roaming is turned on

• ensure you’re in a supported country

**What to expect**

Service should resume once the issue is resolved.
