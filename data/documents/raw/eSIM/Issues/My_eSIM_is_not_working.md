# My eSIM is not working

If your eSIM isn’t working, it may need a quick fix.


**What this means**

This may happen if:

• your device isn’t properly connected

• settings are not configured correctly

**What you should do**

Try:

• restarting your device

• toggling airplane mode on and off

• turning roaming off and on

• checking you’re in a supported country

**What to expect**

These steps resolve most issues.
