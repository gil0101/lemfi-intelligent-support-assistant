# Refunds and conditions for eSIM

You may be eligible for a refund depending on your usage.

**What this means**

Refunds are available for:

• unused and non-activated plans

Refunds are not available for:

• partially used plans

• expired plans

**What to expect**

Refund requests are reviewed within 3–5 business days
