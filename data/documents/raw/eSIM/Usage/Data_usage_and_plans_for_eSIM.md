# Data usage and plans for eSIM

Your eSIM plan gives you a set amount of mobile data for a specific period.

**What this means**

• eSIM is for data only (no calls or SMS)

• plans expire after a set period

• unused data cannot be carried over

**What you should do**

Monitor your usage in the app under **My Plans**.


**What to expect**

• You can top up when your plan expires

• Network speed depends on local carriers
