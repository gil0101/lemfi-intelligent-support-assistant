# What are the limitations of eSIM?

There are some important things to know when using eSIM.


**What this means**

• data only (no calls or texts)

• works only in supported countries

• must be installed on one device per time

• cannot be reused across multiple devices

**What you should do**

Check your plan details before using your eSIM.


**What to expect**

Your experience may vary depending on local network conditions.
