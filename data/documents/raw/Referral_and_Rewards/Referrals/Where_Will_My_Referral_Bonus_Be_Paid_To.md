# Where Will My Referral Bonus Be Paid To?

All referral earnings are paid into your LemFi Cash account (except United States users) when an individual downloads the LemFi app, signs up with your referral code or link, verifies his/her account, and completes a single transfer of 50 GBP, 100 USD, 50 CAD, 50 AUD, or 50 EUR to supported countries in Europe/Africa/Asia/South America (**for non-European users**) or Africa/Asia/South America (**for European users**).
