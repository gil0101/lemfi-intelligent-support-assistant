# How do I track referrals?

To track referrals in the LemFi app, navigate to the Referral section in the top-right corner of the homepage, then click the “Track Your Invites” button. This tab displays your referred users and the bonuses you've earned, which are credited when your invitee completes a qualifying transfer **of 50** AUD, 50 CAD, 50 GBP, 50 EUR or 100 USD.

**What this means**

You’ll be able to see:

• who signed up using your code

• whether they’ve completed the required steps

• rewards you’ve earned

**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Go to the **Referral section**

• View your referral history

**What to expect**

• Each referral will show its progress

• ‘**Invited**’ means the user has not yet qualified for cashback.

• ‘**Completed**’ means the user qualifies for a cashback and may have been paid.

• The bonus will appear once all conditions are met
