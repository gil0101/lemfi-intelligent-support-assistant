# How much do I earn as referral bonus?

You will earn 3**0 AUD, 40 CAD, 25 EUR, 25 GBP, or 20 USD **as a LemFi user registered in Australia**,** Canada, Europe, the United Kingdom**,** or the United States every time an invitee downloads the app; uses your unique referral code or link during sign-up; verifies their account; and initiates a transfer of 50 AUD, 50 CAD, 50 EUR, 50 GBP, or 100 USD through available payment methods in a single transaction to a supported African or Asian country.

The people you invite to the LemFi app will also receive the applicable cashback amount of their sign-up country when they sign up (if in Australia, the United Kingdom, Europe or Canada) or complete a single transfer of the minimum amount of 100 USD. It's a win-win for you, your family, and your friends.
