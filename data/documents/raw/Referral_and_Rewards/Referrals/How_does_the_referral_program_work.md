# How does the referral program work?

LemFi's referral programme allows existing users in Australia, Canada, Europe, the UK, and the US to earn bonuses by inviting friends to sign up and make a first-time transfer. The referrer receives a bonus (e.g., 50 EUR/GBP and 20 USD) when the invitee completes a minimum single transfer. However, the invitee receives their cashback upon signup/verification.

**What this means**

When you share your referral code:

• Your family or friend signs up using your code

• They verify their account

• They complete a qualifying transfer of 50 GBP, 100 USD, 50 CAD, 50 AUD, or 50 EUR in a single cross-border transaction to any of LemFi’s supported countries.

Once these steps are completed, you will receive a bonus. The minimum transfer amount and bonus may vary by country.

**What you should do**

• Share your referral code or link from the app

• Make sure your family or friend completes all required steps

**What to expect**

• Bonuses are only paid after a successful qualifying transfer

• Amounts vary by country and promotion
