# Why didn’t I receive my bonus?

If you didn’t receive your referral bonus, some steps may still be incomplete.

You likely didn't receive your LemFi bonus because the referral requirements weren't fully met: the invitee must verify their account and complete a single qualifying transfer (usually 50 GBP, 50 EUR, 50 AUD, 100 USD, or 50 CAD). The bonus is paid only on the first transaction and must be to a supported country.

**What this means**

• The invitee did not complete KYC/account verification.

• The transfer was less than the required amount (e.g., 50 GBP, 100 USD, or 50 CAD).

• The bonus is only for the first transfer made by the invited person.

• Account issues or breach of LemFi’s Terms of Service.

**What you should do**

• Verify that your account and the friend's account are fully verified.

• Ensure your friend used the correct link.

• Contact LemFi support.

• Use the in-app "Help" section to report the missing bonus

**What to expect**

If all conditions are met, your bonus should be credited automatically. However, if you are yet to receive it after all conditions have been met, please contact us.
