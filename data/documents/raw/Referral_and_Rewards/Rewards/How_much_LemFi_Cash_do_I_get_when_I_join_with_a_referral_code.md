# How much LemFi Cash do I get when I join with a referral code?

The amount depends on the country you sign up from and sometimes on who referred you. They will let you know in their share link and you’ll also see the amount after entering the code when you sign up.

**What this means**

• Your LemFi Cash is always in your home currency

• The amount you get comes from your own sign-up country's offer

• Amounts may vary during special campaigns or promotions

**What to expect**

• Your balance will show immediately in your LemFi Cash Wallet after sign-up

• Please note that LemFi Cash expires — make sure you use it on time!
