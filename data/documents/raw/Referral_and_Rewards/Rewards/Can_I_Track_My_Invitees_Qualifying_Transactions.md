# Can I Track My Invitees’ Qualifying Transactions?

In line with our [privacy policy, referrers are not able to access any referees’ transaction details. This is to ensure confidentiality of each customer’s account and maintain safety of every user’s information.
.
However, you will see the points earned from your invitees’ transfers when credited to your LemFi account. Simply select your “Rewards wallet” on the homepage after login, in order to view the history of points payments on your account.
