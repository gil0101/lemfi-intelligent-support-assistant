# Why didn’t I receive points?

If you didn’t receive points, your transaction might be ineligible.


**What this means**

Points may not be awarded if:

• the transfer does not meet the minimum amount

• the destination is not eligible

• the transfer was fully paid using points

• the transaction was not a qualifying international transfer

**What you should do**

Please ensure that your transaction is eligible for rewards both before and after sending money.


**What to expect**

Points are only awarded for eligible transfers and are added after successful transactions.
