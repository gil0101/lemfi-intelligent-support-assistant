# How Are Points Calculated?

Points are system-generated and depend on the value of an intended qualifying transaction as well as the country where the recipient account is in.

However, point(s) to be earned per qualifying transfers will be displayed on the app — this information is visible on the transfer page where the amount to be sent is inputted, and the final page where your transaction is reviewed before entering your PIN.
