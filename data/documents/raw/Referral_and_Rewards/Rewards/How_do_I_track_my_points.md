# How do I track my points?

You can view your points and rewards in your rewards wallet.


**What this means**

Your rewards wallet shows:

• total points earned

• history of rewards

• points from transfers and referrals


**What you should do**

To view your earned points, please follow these steps:

• Log in to the LemFi app

• Select "Rewards" on the homepage

• Alternatively, you can swipe through your displayed wallets and select 'Rewards."

**What to expect**

• You’ll see your available points balance

The reward values are shown for each qualifying transaction
