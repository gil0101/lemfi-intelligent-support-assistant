# When Will Points Be Remitted To My Account?

If you conduct a qualifying transaction of **at least** 50 GBP/CAD/USD from your LemFi account to applicable countries, the rewards will be paid **as soon as** the transfer is successfully completed.

However, points derived from invitees’ transactions will be credited into the referrer’s wallet on a **weekly** basis.
