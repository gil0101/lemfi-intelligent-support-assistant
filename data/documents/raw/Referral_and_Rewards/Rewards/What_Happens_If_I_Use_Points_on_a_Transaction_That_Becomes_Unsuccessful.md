# What Happens If I Use Points on a Transaction That Becomes Unsuccessful?

If your transaction is cancelled, the points applied on the specific transfer will be returned to your LemFi account. However, rewards displayed on the app before the final stage of the transaction will not be credited, since points are only paid for successful transactions.
