# How To View Points Paid to My Account

To view rewards paid to your LemFi account, simply log into the app and select the “Rewards wallet” icon on the home page — the history of rewards paid for your transactions and those of your invitees’ will be displayed.

Please note that referrers do not have access to view the transaction details of their invitees — only the value of the point paid to his/her account will be visible in the reward wallet.
