# What are the Global account limits?

LemFi global account limits, often referred to as transaction limits or transfer limits, vary based on user verification, currency, and destination.

**What this means**

Limits may apply to:

• How much you can send

• How much you can receive


**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Select a currency wallet

• Tap **View account limits**

**What to expect**

Limits are displayed clearly in the app
