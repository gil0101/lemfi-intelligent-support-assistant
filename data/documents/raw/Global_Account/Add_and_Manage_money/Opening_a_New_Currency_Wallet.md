# Opening a New Currency Wallet

To open a currency wallet on your Global account, simply:

• Log into the LemFi app

• Select "Add money" or the "+" button as seen on the Home Page

• Click "Add New Currency Account" and choose from available currencies (British pounds, United States dollars)

• Complete any additional verification requirements (where applicable)

**Note that the verification process must be successfully completed in order to access the currency wallet feature.**
