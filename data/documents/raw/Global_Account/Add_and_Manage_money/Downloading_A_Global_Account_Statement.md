# Downloading A Global Account Statement

Download the account statement of your available currency wallet(s) using these steps:

• Log into the LemFi app and choose the desired currency wallet (GBP, USD, NGN) on the home page

• Click the "More" button (three dots) at the top-right corner of the screen and select "Account Statement"

• Enter the start date, end date and preferred format (either PDF, or Excel)

• Your statement will be generated and sent to your registered email address
