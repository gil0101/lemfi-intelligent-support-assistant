# How do I view my account details on my Global account?

You can view your account details anytime in the app to receive money.


**What this means**

Each currency wallet (such as GBP or USD) has its own account details that you can share to receive funds.


**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Select your currency wallet (e.g. GBP or USD)

• Tap **Add money**

• Select **Regular bank transfer**

• Your account details will be displayed

You can also tap **Share details** to send them easily.



**What to expect**

• You’ll see the account details needed to receive money

• These details can be shared with the sender

Note:

**NGN wallets do not currently have account details for direct bank transfers**
