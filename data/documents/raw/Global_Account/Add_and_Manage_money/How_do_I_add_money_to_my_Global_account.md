# How do I add money to my Global account?

You can add money to your Global Account using bank transfers.


**What this means**

You can fund your account by adding money to your currency wallets (such as GBP or USD).


**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Select your currency wallet

• Tap **Add money**

• Choose **Regular bank transfer**

• Use the displayed account details to complete the transfer

Make sure the sender uses the correct account details

**What to expect**

• Funds will be credited once the transfer is completed

• USD deposits may take **1–2 business days** to reflect

**Next steps**

To view your account details, check your wallet and select **Add money**.
