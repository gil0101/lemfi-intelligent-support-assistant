# Where can I send money from my Global Account?

You can send money from a LemFi Global Account (GBP/USD wallets) to over 30 countries across Africa, Asia, Europe, and South America, as well as between your currency wallets. Key destinations include Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Senegal, Cameroon, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and various European countries.

**What this means**

You can send money from your GBP or USD wallets to supported countries across the following:

• Africa: Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Tanzania, Cameroon, Benin, Burkina Faso, Congo, Egypt, Ethiopia, Gabon, Liberia, Mali, Morocco, Tunisia, Togo.

• Asia: China, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Vietnam.

• Europe: Belgium, France, the Netherlands, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Portugal, and Spain.

• South America: Brazil.

**What you should do**

Start a transfer in the app to see the full list of supported countries and options.

• Log in to the LemFi app.

• Click "Send Money" on the home page.

• Select the sending currency (GBP/USD) and the receiving country.

• Input the amount and choose the recipient.

• Select "Wallet Balance" as the payment method.

• Enter your PIN to authorize the transaction.


**What to expect**

Global accounts (specifically for users in Nigeria receiving foreign currency) also allow converting funds between wallets or transferring to other NGN wallets.

• You’ll see available destinations before confirming your transfer

Fees, exchange rates, and delivery times are shown upfront
