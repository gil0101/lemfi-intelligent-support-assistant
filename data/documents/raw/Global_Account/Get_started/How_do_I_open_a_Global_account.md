# How do I open a Global account?

Setting up a Global Account is quick if you meet the requirements: download the LemFi app (Android 6.0+/iOS 14.0+), select your country, and register with your legal details.

**What this means**

The Global Account is currently available to eligible users in Nigeria.

To open one, you must:

• be 18 years or older

• have a Nigerian phone number (+234)

• have a Nigerian residential address

• complete BVN verification

• Provide a valid international passport

**What you should do**

• Download the app and select **Nigeria**

• Enter your phone number and verify it

• Fill in your personal details

• Complete email and BVN verification

• Submit your required document for ID verification

**What to expect**

• Most accounts are set up within minutes

In some cases, it may take longer for verification
