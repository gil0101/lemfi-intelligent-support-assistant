# How do I verify my Global account?

You’ll need to complete identity verification to use your Global Account.


**What this means**

Verification ensures your account is secure and meets regulatory requirements.


**What you should do**

To verify your Global Account on LemFi:

• Log in to the app and select "Verify Email,"

• Input your Nigerian residential address,

• Submit your Bank Verification Number (BVN)

• complete identity verification using a Nigerian International Passport and a selfie. Verification usually takes less than 5 minutes.

• Ensure your details are accurate and match your documents

**What to expect**

• Verification usually takes a few minutes

• In some cases, it may take up to 24 hours

You’ll be notified once verification is complete
