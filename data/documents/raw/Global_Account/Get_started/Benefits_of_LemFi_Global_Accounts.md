# Benefits of LemFi Global Accounts

As a Nigerian resident, you have access to the following features on the LemFi global account:

1. Hold Multiple Currencies

Open separate wallets for U.S. dollar (USD), and British pound (GBP) and keep money in them, without having multiple bank accounts for each currency. Switch between these wallets seamlessly, within one app.

2. Send Money Across Borders

Transfer funds from your GBP or USD wallets to recipients in more than thirty countries across Africa, Europe, Asia, and South America.

3. Receive Payment

With LemFi Global accounts, you can receive your remuneration or payment for services rendered in your LemFi account, when funds are sent into your unique pounds and dollars bank details (sort code and account number for GBP, routing and account number for USD), within a set timeframe for each currency.

4. Currency Conversion

Convert GBP to USD in real-time, or vice-versa, in accordance with supported currencies — exchange money between your wallets at competitive rates!
