# How do I manage my card (freeze, security)?

You can manage your LemFi card (freeze or unfreeze) directly in the LemFi app to help keep your credit account secure.


**What this means**

• Log in to your LemFi account.

• Go to the "Cards" section, which is usually on the home page or under the profile menu.

• Select your active card.

• Select "Freeze" or "Disable" to freeze or disable the card. This instantly pauses online purchases by taking action if you think your card has been compromised.

• You can instantly lift the freeze in the same section of the app if you find your card.


**What to expect**

• Freezing your card can help prevent new transactions

• You can use in-app security controls to keep your account protected
