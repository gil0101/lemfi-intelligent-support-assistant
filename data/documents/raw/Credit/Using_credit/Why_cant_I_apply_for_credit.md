# Why can’t I apply for credit?

If you can’t apply for LemFi Credit, it may be because the feature isn't available to you or you don't meet the eligibility requirements.


**What this means**

LemFi Credit is currently available only to eligible customers in the United Kingdom.

You may be unable to apply if:

• the feature is not yet available on your app

• you do not meet the eligibility criteria

• additional checks are still required

**What you should do**

Check that you:

• are 18 or older

• are based in the UK

• have an eligible UK bank account

• have completed the required setup and verification


**What to expect**

In some cases, the option to apply may be temporarily unavailable even if you may become eligible later.
