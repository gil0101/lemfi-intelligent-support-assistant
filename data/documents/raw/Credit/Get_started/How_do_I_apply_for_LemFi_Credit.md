# How do I apply for LemFi Credit?

To apply for a LemFi Credit card (Send Now, Pay Later), download the LemFi app, verify your identity, and apply within the "Cards" section.


**What this means**

• After installing the LemFi app on the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, register with your legal name, address, and phone number.

• Select the credit option in the app to check your eligibility; it does not affect your credit score.

• Securely link your bank account to verify your monthly income and financial history.

• Upon approval, review the credit offer and sign the agreement.

• You can activate your virtual card to start using your credit line.


**What you should do**

• Enter your personal details (name, address history)

• Provide employment and income information

• Connect your bank account via Open Banking

• Review and sign the agreement


**What to expect**

• Most applications are processed quickly

• Some may require additional checks

• You’ll be notified once a decision is made
