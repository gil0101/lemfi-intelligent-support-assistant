# Who can apply for LemFi Credit?

LemFi credit is primarily available to UK residents aged 18+ who have an active, verified LemFi account, a satisfactory credit score, and utilize the app for international transfers.

**What this means**

To apply, you must:

• be 18 years or older

• be a resident of the United Kingdom

• Be an active, verified LemFi remittance user.

• have an active UK bank account that supports Open Banking

• have a bank account active for at least 3 months


**What you should do**

Make sure your account and financial details are up to date before applying.


**What to expect**

Eligibility is assessed based on your financial profile and credit history.
