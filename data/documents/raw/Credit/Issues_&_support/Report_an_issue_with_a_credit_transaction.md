# Report an issue with a credit transaction

If something doesn’t look right with a credit transaction, let us know as soon as possible.


**What this means**

You may need help if:

• you don’t recognise a transaction

• a payment was made in error

• there’s a problem with a purchase


**What you should do**

Share the following:

• the transaction details

• what went wrong

• any relevant screenshots or supporting information


**What to expect**

Our team will review the transaction and guide you on the next steps.
