# What happens if I miss a payment?

Missing a payment on LemFi Credit can result in a negative impact on your credit score, as late payments are reported to credit reference agencies. While LemFi aims to be transparent with no late fees, persistent non-payment can lead to the suspension or closure of your account and potential debt collection actions.


**What this means**

• Missed or late payments are reported, which can damage your credit history.

• LemFi may restrict access to the app and close your account if you fail to pay any outstanding amounts.

• Once an account is closed due to non-payment, you will no longer be able to use the service.

• You still owe the balance, and if not paid, further actions to recover the debt may be taken.


**What you should do**

Make your payment as soon as possible and review your repayment method in the app. If available, set up automatic payments to help avoid missed repayments in future.


**What to expect**

• Your account may remain active, but missed payments can affect your repayment history

You may need to repay overdue payments before continuing to use your credit
