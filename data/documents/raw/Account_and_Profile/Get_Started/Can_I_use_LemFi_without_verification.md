# Can I use LemFi without verification?

You may use some basic features without verifying your identity, but your access will be limited.

**What this means**

Without completing verification:

• you may have restricted access to certain features

• your transaction limits may be lower

• some services may not be available

**What you should do**

Complete identity verification to unlock full access to your account.


**What to expect**

Once verified:

• you’ll be able to use all available features

• your limits may increase
