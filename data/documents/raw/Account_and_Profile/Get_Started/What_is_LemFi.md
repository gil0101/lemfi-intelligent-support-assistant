# What is LemFi?

LemFi is a financial app that helps you send, receive, and manage money across countries. LemFi enables international money transfers from Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, the United States, and certain European countries to Africa, Asia, and other parts of the world. Our mission is to alleviate difficulties experienced by people who relocate and provide adequate financial services that allow immigrants to truly participate in the global economy, regardless of their locations.

The best part is that we allow you to send money at little or no fees within minutes, depending on the country you send money to, at affordable rates, thus ensuring both efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

Supported countries for transfers on our platform include:

•
**AFRICA: **Benin Republic, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Congo, Egypt, Ethiopia, Gabon, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Kenya, Liberia, Mali, Morocco, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, Tanzania, Tunisia, Togo, Uganda,

•
**ASIA: **China, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Vietnam

•
**EUROPE: **Belgium, France, Netherlands, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Portugal and Spain

•
**SOUTH AMERICA:** Brazil


**What this means**

You can use LemFi to:

• send money internationally

• manage your wallet

• access additional financial services (depending on your location)

You cannot use LemFi to:

• receive a refund from a merchant or vendor

• purchase cryptocurrency

**What you should do**

To get started, [download the app and create an account. You’ll be guided through a simple setup process, including identity verification.
