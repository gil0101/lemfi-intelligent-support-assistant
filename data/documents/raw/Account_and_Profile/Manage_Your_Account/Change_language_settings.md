# Change language settings

You can switch the app language to your preferred language from the available languages below.
- Chinese
- English (UK)
- English (US)
- French
- German
- Italian
- Portugese
- Spanish


**What this means**

Language settings affect how content is displayed in the app.


**What you should do**

To select your preferred language.

• Log in to the app

• At the top left corner, click on My Accounts

• Select Account Information

• Click on Language and proceed to select the preferred language

• Click on "Continue" to save

**What to expect**

Changes apply instantly.


**Next steps**

Restart the app if changes don’t appear immediately.
