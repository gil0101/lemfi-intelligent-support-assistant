# Change your email address

To update the email address associated with your LemFi account, please follow the steps below:

•

• Log in to the LemFi app.

• Tap the profile icon located at the top left corner of the screen.

• Select **Account Information**.

• Tap **Update Email Address**.

• Complete the facial verification process when prompted.

• Enter your new email address in the space provided.

• A verification link will be sent to the new email address.

• Open your inbox and click on the **Verify Email** button to complete the update.



Please note that they may receive an OTP on their registered phone number during the process. If prompted, they should enter the OTP in the space provided to continue.
