# Change your home address

It is important to keep your contact details up to date with LemFi. If you have moved, you can update your home address by contacting our support team directly.

You may be asked to provide proof of address (e.g., a utility bill or bank statement) if you are changing your country of residence, as LemFi complies with KYC and regulatory standards.
