# Turn notifications on or off

You can enable or disable notifications in the app.


**What this means**

Get real-time alerts in the app for promotions and offers from LemFi, exciting new features and services, and transaction details. This also allows you to instantly spot and report unauthorized transactions. Track your transactions instantly so you know when money leaves or enters your account.

**What you should do**

Go to **Settings** and adjust your notification preferences.

To manage your notifications, follow the steps below:

- Log in to your LemFi app.

-Tap the profile icon located at the top-left corner of the screen.

-Select “Notification Preferences.”

-Toggle off any notifications you no longer wish to receive.


**What to expect**

Changes apply immediately.


**Next steps**

Keeping your alerts enabled is recommended to be duly informed of all necessary updates.
