#  How do I delete a beneficiary

• Log in to your LemFi account

• Tap the green "Send Money" button on your home page

• Enter an amount in the "You Send" field and select the recipient's currency by tapping the flag under "Recipient Gets"

• Tap "Change" on the "Paying With" field

• Click "Continue"

• Your saved beneficiaries for transfers to the selected recipient country will appear on the next page.

To delete a single beneficiary, swipe left on the beneficiary you would like to remove.

To delete multiple beneficiaries, tap the button next to "Your Recipients" — an icon will appear beside each saved beneficiary. Simply tap the icon next to anyone you would like to delete.
