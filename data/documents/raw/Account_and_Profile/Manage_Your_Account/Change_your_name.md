# Change your name

You will be unable to directly edit your legal name in the app once your identity has been verified. To change your name (e.g., due to marriage or legal correction), you will need to contact our customer support team to initiate a Know Your Customer (KYC) update.

You will be required to submit official proof of the name change, such as:

• A marriage certificate

• An updated government-issued ID (Passport, Driver’s License, or National ID).

The team will review your documents and update your profile. This process is required for all users to comply with financial regulations and keep their accounts secure.
