# Change your date of birth

Because the date of birth is a critical part of your identity verification (KYC), it cannot be updated directly within the app profile settings. To correct your date of birth, you must contact the LemFi Support Team to update it on your behalf.

To speed up the process, you will be required to provide a clear copy of the correct birth certificate. Our team will verify the documents submitted. Once verified, we will update your profile. You may be asked to re-verify your identity to reflect the changes.
