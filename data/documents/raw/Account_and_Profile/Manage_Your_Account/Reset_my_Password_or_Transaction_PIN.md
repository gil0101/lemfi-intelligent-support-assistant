# Reset my Password or Transaction PIN

If you’ve forgotten your password or transaction PIN, you can easily reset it.


**What this means**

Resetting your credentials helps you regain access securely.


**What you should do**

If you are unable to access your account because you forgot your password,

Open the LemFi app and select "Log In."

• Open the LemFi app and select "Log In."

• 'Click "Recover account."

• If your credentials have been previously saved, select "Recover Account" on the login page

• Choose "Forgot your password."

• Follow the steps as displayed on the app

If you are able to access your account, you can change your password and transaction PIN;

• Click the profile icon at the top-left corner of the page

• Select “Security and privacy."

• Click "Change Password"

• Enter your new password and click on "Continue."

**What to expect**

You’ll need to verify your identity as part of the resetting process if you are unable to access your account
