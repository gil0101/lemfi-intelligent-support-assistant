# What happens to my balance if my account is closed?

Your remaining balance will be reviewed during the closure process.


**What this means**

Depending on why your account is closed,  your account funds may be returned, withdrawn, or held before closure is completed.


**What to expect**

You’ll receive guidance during the closure process.
