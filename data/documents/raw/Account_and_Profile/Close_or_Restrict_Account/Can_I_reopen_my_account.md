# Can I reopen my account?

In cases where you had previously requested an account closure, you may be able to reopen your account.



**What this means**

The reason for your account closure and the team's internal review determine whether you can reactivate it. If your account was closed due to inactivity or a simple request, LemFi can generally reopen it, provided there are no ongoing regulatory or security reasons for keeping it closed.


**What to expect**

Be prepared to go through the verification process to prove your identity. Approval is not guaranteed.


**Next steps**

Be patient and await feedback from the support team.
