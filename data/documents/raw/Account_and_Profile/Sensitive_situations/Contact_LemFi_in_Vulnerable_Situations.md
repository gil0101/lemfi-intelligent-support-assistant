# Contact LemFi in Vulnerable Situations

While we note that a user may feel reluctant to share concerns in vulnerable situations, LemFI offers various means of communication, thus allowing a range of 24/7 support in these moments. The following channels should be used to contact our dedicated team:

•
**Email**: Raise a request directly from the LemFi app or [Contact Form

•
**In-App Chat**: Simply click “Help and Support" on the Settings options, select “Visit Help Centre” and continue with the chat icon displayed

•
**Phone**: Various dedicated call lines are listed on the [Contact Us page



LemFi may render other forms of assistance depending on the customer's requirement — these may include (but are not limited to):

• Written communication in multiple languages upon request

• Calmer communication pace, especially for sensitive topics

• A continuous point of contact, **where **appropriate



Rest assured that your information will be handled with care and confidentiality.
