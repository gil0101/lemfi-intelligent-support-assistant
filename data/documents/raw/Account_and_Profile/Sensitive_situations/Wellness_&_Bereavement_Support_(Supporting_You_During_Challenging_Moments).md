# Wellness & Bereavement Support (Supporting You During Challenging Moments)

We understand that certain situations may affect one’s finances and the manner in which a customer engages with the app. Such moments could cause him/her to feel vulnerable — either emotionally, mentally, or physically — depending on events happening at the time. Other circumstances that may be difficult to live with are bereavement and the demise of a loved one.

Such seasons can be rather difficult. Nonetheless, LemFi is available to provide assistance, whether temporarily and permanently, in accordance with set guidelines, as well as address your inquiries with compassion and respect during these moments.

If you are in Canada, Europe, the United Kingdom, or the United States and require assistance during a difficult situation, kindly contact any of the following charitable organizations for additional information:
