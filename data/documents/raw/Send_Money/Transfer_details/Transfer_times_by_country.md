# Transfer times by country

Transfer times vary depending on where you’re sending money.

**Typical delivery time**

**Transfer Times by Country**

**Africa**

• **Benin Republic** (Mobile Wallet) – Real-time, 24/7.

• **Burkina Faso** (Mobile Wallet) – Real-time, 24/7.

• **Cameroon **(Mobile Wallet) – Real-time, 24/7.

• **Congo **– Real-time, 24/7.

• **Egypt **– Transfers are processed during bank hours (8:30 AM – 3 PM, Sunday–Thursday).

• **Ethiopia **– Transfers take 1 business day. Example: sent on Monday → arrives Tuesday.

• **Gambia **– Transfers are currently unavailable.

• **Ghana **– Real-time, 24/7.

• **Ivory Coast** – GTB & Mobile Wallet: real-time; other banks: 1 business day.

• **Mali **– Transfers take 1 business day (working days only).

• **Morocco **– Transfers sent before 4:30 PM arrive the same day; after 4:30 PM, the next day.

• **Nigeria **– Real-time, 24/7.

• **Togo **– Real-time, 24/7.

• **Tunisia **– Transfers take 3 business days.

**Asia**

• **Bangladesh **– Before 3 PM: same day; after 3 PM: next business day. Weekends: Friday–Saturday.

• **China **– Weekdays only: WeChat up to 2 business days, Alipay up to 3 business days.

• **India **– Real-time, 24/7.

• **Nepal **– Mobile Wallet & instant banks: real-time; SBI & Standard Chartered Bank: processed during bank hours.

• **Pakistan **– IBFT: real-time; after hours or weekends: 2 business days.

• **Philippines **– ≤ PHP 50,000: real-time; > PHP 50,000 before 3 PM: same day, after 3 PM: next business day.

• **Sri Lanka **– Bank transfers: real-time, 24/7.

• **Vietnam **– NAPAS & Mobile Wallet: real-time; other banks 2–4 hours during working hours, next business day after hours.

**North America**

•
**USA **– Transfer to US bank accounts: Within 1-2 business days.


**What can affect delivery time**

• bank processing hours

• weekends and public holidays

• service or partner downtime

**Before you send**

You’ll always see the estimated delivery time in the app before confirming your transfer.
