# How do I send money?

Sending money is quick and straightforward — you can transfer money to various locations within Africa, Asia, Europe, and South America in two ways:

• Sending from a funded wallet (for the U.K., U.S., and Canada only)

• Sending without a LemFi wallet (applicable to all sign-up countries)

Depending on your country of registration, different locations may appear as receiving countries in the transfer section of the app. Log in to the app, click on “Send money” > click the flag beside “Recipient gets”.

**What this means**

You can send money internationally directly from your app using your wallet or selected payment method.


**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Tap “Send money”

• Enter the amount

• Select or add a recipient

• Review the details

• Confirm the transfer

**What to expect**

Before confirming, you’ll see:

• the total amount you’ll pay

• the exchange rate

• any fees

• the estimated delivery time

After sending, you can track your transfer in the Transactions section.

To do so, open the LemFi app on your mobile device > Tap "**Transactions**" located at the bottom of the home screen > Select a specific transaction to view its full details, such as the amount, date, and current status.
