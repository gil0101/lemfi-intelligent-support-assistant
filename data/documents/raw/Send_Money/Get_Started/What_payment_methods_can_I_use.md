# What payment methods can I use?

LemFi supports several payment methods for funding wallets and sending money, varying by region (UK, US, Canada). Common methods include Debit Cards, Bank Transfers, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Canadian users can use Interac, while US and UK users can utilise dedicated bank transfer options to fund their accounts.

• Bank Transfers: Available for funding GBP and USD wallets via local bank transfers.

• Debit Cards: Widely supported in the US and UK for instant funding.

• Apple/Google Pay: Supported across all major regions for quick transactions.

• Interac: Specifically for Canadian users.

• P2P (Peer-to-Peer): Allows users to send money between wallets.

**What this means**

Available payment methods may include:

• United Kingdom: Debit card, instant bank transfer, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

• United States: Debit card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

• Canada: Interac, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

• Europe: Payments can be made directly from a card, bank account, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

**What you should do**

Start a transfer in the app and check the **Paying with** section to see the payment methods available to you.

To do so, click on “**Send money**”  > Enter Transfer Details: Select your sending and receiving currencies and input the desired amount to send. > Tap on the "**Paying with**" > Choose Your Method: Select your preferred payment option from the list of supported methods shown for your account and destination.

**What to expect**

You’ll only see the payment methods that are supported for your account and transfer.

Some methods may be instant, while others may take longer to process.
