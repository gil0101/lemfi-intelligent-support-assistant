# How much can I send?

LemFi transfer limits vary by user, currency, and destination country, and you may need to check your personalized limits directly in the app under "Account information" on the homepage. While specific limits vary, they are designed for international transfers from the UK, EU, or Canada to countries in Africa and Asia and may incur a fee.

**What this means**

Your transfer limit may vary based on:

• Your verification level

• Your location

• Regulatory requirements

**What to expect**

If your transfer exceeds the limit:

• You may need to send a smaller amount

• Complete additional verification
