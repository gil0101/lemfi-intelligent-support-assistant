# Where can I send money?

You can send money to a range of countries supported by the app. LemFi allows users in Canada, Europe, the UK, and the USA to send money to various countries in Africa, Asia, and other regions, with fast transfers usually completed in minutes.

Key destination countries include:

Africa: Benin Republic, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Congo, Egypt, Ethiopia, Gabon, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Kenya, Liberia, Mali, Morocco, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, Tanzania, Togo, Tunisia, and Uganda.

Asia: Bangladesh, China, India, Nepal, Pakistan, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, and Vietnam.

Europe: Belgium, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, and Spain.

South America: Brazil.

Asia: China, India, Pakistan, and the Philippines.


**What this means**

You can send funds using a funded wallet or directly via bank transfer or debit card, depending on the available methods:

• your location

• local regulations

• supported payout methods

**What you should do**

Start a transfer in the app to see the full list of available countries.

To do so, click on “Send money”  > click the flag beside “Recipient gets”
