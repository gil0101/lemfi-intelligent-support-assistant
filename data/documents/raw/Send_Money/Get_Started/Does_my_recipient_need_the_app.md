# Does my recipient need the app?

No, your recipient does not need the LemFi app to receive money. LemFi allows you to send funds directly to their local bank account or mobile money wallet.

Bank/Mobile Money Transfers: Funds are deposited directly into their existing bank account, so they do not need to download any app.

WeChat/Alipay: If sending via WeChat or Alipay, they will need to authorize the transaction in the respective app the first time.

Peer-to-Peer (P2P): If you are sending to another LemFi user (P2P), they will need the app to receive it instantly.


**What this means**

You can send money directly to:

• a bank account

• a mobile wallet

Your recipient doesn’t need to sign up.


**What you should do**

Just make sure your recipient's details are correct when sending money.


**What to expect**

Once the transfer is completed:

• The money is delivered directly to their account

No action is required from your recipient
