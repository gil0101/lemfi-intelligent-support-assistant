# What exchange rate will I get?

LemFi offers competitive, real-time exchange rates, often with zero transfer fees for many currencies, though these vary by currency and change throughout the day. Rates are not fixed, so it is essential to check the specific rate in the app before sending money.


**What this means**

The exchange rate determines how much your recipient receives in their local currency.

Use the App: Log in to the LemFi app and tap "Send Money" to see the real-time rate for your currency pair.

Verify Before Sending: The rate shown when initiating the transaction is the rate you will receive.


**What to expect**

Check the exchange rate shown before confirming your transfer. Many transactions, particularly to African countries, have zero transfer fees. LemFi aims for competitive rates that reflect market conditions, and exchange rates can fluctuate throughout the day, so it is recommended to check before confirming any transfer.
