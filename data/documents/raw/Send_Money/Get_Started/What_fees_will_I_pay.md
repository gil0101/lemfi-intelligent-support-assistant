# What fees will I pay?

LemFi offers zero transfer fees on many routes, including to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, etc. However, some transactions, such as those to Bangladesh, China, India, Nepal, and Pakistan, may incur a fee.

Also, while the first transfer to a new country is typically free, subsequent transfers may attract fees.


**What this means**

Some transfers may include a fee depending on the following:

• the destination country

• the transfer amount

**What you should do**

Always review the transfer summary before confirming your payment.


**What to expect**

Key fee information:

• Transfer Fees: Zero for many receiving countries.

• Deposits: No fees are charged for funding your wallet.

• Variable Fees: Some destinations may charge a fee based on the country, which is shown in the app during the transaction.

Always confirm the final exchange rate and any applicable fees shown in the LemFi app before completing your transfer.
