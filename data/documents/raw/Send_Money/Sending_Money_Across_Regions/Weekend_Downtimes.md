# Weekend Downtimes

Processing timelines may be affected during weekends in certain countries. Transactions initiated during these periods will be processed once the weekend ends. Note the weekend downtimes for the following countries:

Bangladesh - Friday & Saturday
Pakistan - Saturday & Sunday
