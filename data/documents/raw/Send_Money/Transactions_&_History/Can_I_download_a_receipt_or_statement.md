# Can I download a receipt or statement?

Yes — you can download a receipt for individual transactions and access your account statement.


**What this means**

Receipts and statements provide a record of your transactions for your reference.


**What you should do**

**To download a receipt:**

• Open the app

• Go to **Transactions**

• Select a transaction

• Tap “**Download”** in front of Statement

**What to expect**

Your receipt will include:

• transaction details

• amount sent or received

• fees and exchange rate

• date and time

**Next steps**

You can use receipts for:

• proof of payment

• personal records
