# How do I view my transactions?

You can view all your past transactions directly in the app. To view your transactions on LemFi, log in to the app and check your transaction history directly on the homepage, or navigate to your profile to download a full account statement.

**What this means**

Your transaction history shows all your activity, including:

• money sent

• money received

• wallet activity

**What you should do**

• Open the app

• Tap **Transactions**

• Browse or select a transaction for more details

**What to expect**

• You’ll see a list of your recent transactions

• Each transaction includes key details like amount, status, and date
