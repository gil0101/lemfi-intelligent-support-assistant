# Can I delete my transaction history?

No, you cannot directly delete your transaction history on LemFi. As a regulated financial institution, LemFi is obligated to retain personal and transactional data for safety, security, and legal compliance, often for a minimum of 7 years. While you can close your account, they cannot delete all of your data.

Transaction records cannot be deleted, but inactive accounts may have some information removed. Before closing, withdraw your balance. LemFi is mandated to keep records for fraud prevention and legal reasons, as outlined in their [Privacy Notice.
