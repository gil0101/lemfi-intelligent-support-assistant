# My transfer is still "In Progress"

If your transfer is showing as **in progress**, it means it’s still being handled — this is usually normal.


**What “In Progress” means**

Your transfer is:

• being reviewed

• sent through payment partners

• or waiting to be received by the destination bank

**How long will this take**

Most transfers are completed within the expected timeframe displayed on the app when the transaction is initiated, but the duration can vary depending on the destination country and bank.

**What you should do**

No action is needed in most cases. Simply check the status in your app.
