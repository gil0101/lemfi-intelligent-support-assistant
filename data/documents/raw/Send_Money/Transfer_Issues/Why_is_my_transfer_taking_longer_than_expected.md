# Why is my transfer taking longer than expected?

We understand delays can be worrying. In most cases, your transfer is still in progress but needs slightly more time.

**Common reasons for delays**

Transfers may take longer due to:

• bank processing times

• weekends or public holidays

• temporary service or partner delays

• additional checks for security

**What you can do**

• Check your transfer status in the app

• Compare it with the estimated delivery time

• Allow extra time during weekends or holidays for your transactions to Bangladesh, China, Egypt, Ivory Coast, India, Pakistan, Philippines and Vietnam.
