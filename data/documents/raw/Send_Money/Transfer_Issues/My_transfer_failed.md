# My transfer failed

If your transfer fails, don’t worry — your money is safe. Sometimes transfers fail due to different factors.

**Why transfers fail**

Such failures can happen due to:

• The transfer is being initiated outside local banking hours

• The transfer is being sent during a public holiday, weekend, or bank holiday

• Your recipient account has limitations

• Your recipient bank or our payment partner is experiencing a temporary downtime

**What happens next**

If a transaction is unsuccessful, you'll receive a refund that's automatically returned to your wallet. However, if you signed up from a European country, the money will be returned to the source account.


**How long do refunds take**

Refunds are usually processed:

• Instantly to your LemFi wallet,

• 3–5 business days, to your debit card

• 1–2 business days to your bank account

**What you can do after your transfer fails**

Please verify your beneficiary details and account limits, then try the transfer again.
